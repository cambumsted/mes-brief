Marketing Effectiveness Solution

A self-service hub where DDS leadership can answer the recurring, job-critical questions they get asked every cycle — without chasing channel owners or stitching reports together.

The Problem The Deliverable The Timeline
01 · The Problem

Leadership doesn't have one place to answer the questions they're paid to answer.

Asked every cycle

01
How should I invest incremental marketing dollars?
When budget opens up — or gets pulled.
02
What channel is driving this trend?
Traffic, conversion, revenue — which lever moved it?
03
What's the ROI of this partnership?
NFL, AMEX (Americas / Asia / EMEA), and others.

Today, answering any one means

Chase channel & region owners
→
Pull reports manually
→
Reconcile definitions
→
Answer arrives late — or not at all

Three answer surfaces in V1

Q1 · Investment
Where do I put incremental dollars?
Cross-channel cost-per-outcome & ROI ranked across channels and LOBs, with proxies where ROI isn't the goal.
Sources Triweekly · Exec Monthly · Partnership grid · CJA / Power BI links
Q2 · Trend driver
What moved the number?
Trend by channel × LOB, MoM & YoY pre-flagged. Click a channel → pre-filtered Adobe / CJA workspace.
Sources Manual chart · Saved CJA / Adobe Analytics workspaces
Q3 · Partnership ROI
Is the spend earning its keep?
The partnership comparability grid as the front door — same KPIs across regions, side by side.
Sources Regional reports · Partner dashboards · Back-end platforms

What's behind every answer

A · Curated

Manually curated reports

Cross-channel reads no single tool produces. Compiled by DDS on a known cadence, dropped in as living pages.

  • Efficiency snapshot · Partnership grid · GTM moment rollup
  • Cadence: monthly headline · quarterly partnership
B · Direct links

Pre-segmented dashboards

Live tools, pre-filtered to the slice that matches the question — no reconfiguring on landing.

  • Tools: Adobe Analytics · CJA · Power BI
  • Filters: LOB · region · channel · window · GTM moment
In scope · V1
  • The three questions, with curated answer surfaces + pre-filtered dashboard links
  • Surface Consumer first — clear path to add SMB · Gaming · M365
  • Manual refresh — monthly headline, quarterly partnership
Not in scope · V1
  • A new BI tool or replacement for channel-specific reports
  • Real-time data — follows source-system cadence
  • Automated cross-channel attribution (Run phase)
