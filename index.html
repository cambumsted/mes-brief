Performance Marketing Hub

Marketing Effectiveness Solution

One destination where DDS leadership can see what marketing is doing across the funnel, how it's performing, and what business impact it's driving.

The Problem The Deliverable The Timeline
01 · The Problem

We can't see marketing as one connected story.

What we don't have today
  • No single destinationOne place that ties together what we're doing across the funnel.
  • Partnership performance lives in silosAsia, Americas, and EMEA report regionally with no consolidated view.
  • Outbound activity isn't tied to GTM momentsBack-to-School, Holiday, Cyber Week — we can't roll up the marketing push behind each one.
  • Web tracking hasn't kept paceOCID generation and channel taxonomies don't reflect how we actually market today.
  • Unknown unknowns by channelNo inventory of the questions each channel can't currently answer about itself.
  • ROI is patchySeveral channels still rely on proxy or anecdotal evidence for impact.
What this costs us
  • Reactive investment decisionsMade channel-by-channel, not as a portfolio.
  • Partnership dollars allocated by inertiaNo shared baseline to compare across regions.
  • Marketing's contribution gets undersoldWe can't roll up impact against the GTM moments we support.
  • Leadership feels in the darkLimited visibility into what's running, how it's performing, and where to invest when budget opens up.
02 · The Deliverable

One destination, full funnel reporting.

What we're doing across the funnel

Activity at each funnel stage — tied to goals, outbound channels, and GTM campaigns. Answers: what's running, for whom, and toward what moment?

How it's performing at both leadership and channel-lead depth

Two reading depths from the same data: headline KPIs for leadership and deeper channel KPIs for channel leads.

The business impact it's delivering

ROI roll-up by channel & activation. Where ROI isn't the goal (e.g., upper & mid funnel), expected outcomes are clearly defined (e.g., traffic).
