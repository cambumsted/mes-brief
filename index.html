What's behind every answer

A · Curated Manually curated reports ▾

Cross-channel reads no single tool produces today. Compiled by the DDS team on a known cadence and dropped into the hub as living pages.

  • Examples: cross-channel efficiency snapshot, partnership grid, GTM moment rollup
  • Cadence: monthly headline · quarterly partnership
  • Sources: Reports inventory (Paid Media, Partnership, Cross-channel)
B · Direct links Pre-segmented dashboard links ▾

Live tools, pre-filtered to the slice that matches the question — so leaders don't land on a generic dashboard and have to reconfigure it.

  • Tools: Adobe Analytics · CJA · Power BI
  • Pre-applied filters: LOB · region · channel · time window · GTM moment
  • Each link labeled with what it answers and what it doesn't
In scope · V1
  • The four questions above, with curated answer surfaces + pre-filtered dashboard links
  • Surface Consumer first — clear path to add SMB · Gaming · M365
  • Manual refresh — monthly headline, quarterly partnership
Not in scope · V1
  • A new BI tool or replacement for channel-specific reports
  • Real-time data — follows source-system cadence
  • Automated cross-channel attribution (Run phase)
