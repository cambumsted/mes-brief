Pillar 1 · Investment & ROI

Spend, return, and the ratio leadership cares about.

Roll up paid media, partnership co-marketing, events, and owned channels into a single ROI view — with finance reconciliation alongside, not in a separate deck.

  • Spend mix by channel, partner, and region
  • ROI vs. target, with trend over time
  • Drill from total spend down to a single line item

Pillar 2 · Performance Trends

The MBR view — but live, every day.

The information that lives in the monthly review, surfaced continuously so leadership can react to leading indicators instead of trailing ones.

  • Pipeline, engagement, and conversion trends
  • Auto-flagged anomalies and inflection points
  • Compare regions, products, or time periods side-by-side

Pillar 3 · Campaign Performance

From "Back-to-School" down to a single asset.

The view we don't have today: a roll-up at the GTM moment level — not the channel level — so we can finally say "Outbound Marketing drove $X for Back-to-School."

  • Moment view (Back-to-School, Surface Launch, Holiday, etc.)
  • Channel and asset contribution to each moment
  • Pipeline attribution from tactical execution to outcome

Pillar 4 · Partnership Performance

Cross-portfolio visibility, not regional silos.

Today partnership performance rarely escapes its region of origin. This view rolls every partnership across every region into one comparable lens — so portfolio decisions stop being guesswork.

  • Leaderboard across regions and partner tiers
  • Spend, pipeline, and ROI per partner
  • Trend direction (rising / steady / declining)

Under the hood

Stitches what we already have. Doesn't replace it.

The solution sits on top of existing channel reports, GTM trackers, and finance data — adding the integration and roll-up layer we don't have today.

→

Inputs

Paid media reports, partnership decks, MBR data, GTM campaign trackers, finance reconciliation.

⊕

Integration layer

Common taxonomy, shared definitions, consistent time/region dimensions. The "stitching" we don't have today.

↑

Outputs

Leadership home page, four pillar views, drilldowns, and exportable snapshots for MBR/QBR.

What we'd build

Light enough to ship; structured enough to scale.

Not a new system of record — a presentation layer that pulls from the systems already in use. Tool selection (Power BI, Fabric, custom) is downstream of agreeing on the views.

  • Re-uses existing data sources
  • Owned by DDS, designed for leadership
  • Can be replaced or upgraded without disrupting source teams

What we need from you.

Reactions to the shape: which views matter most, what's missing, and where leadership would actually start. These wireframes exist to make the conversation concrete, not to lock in the design.
