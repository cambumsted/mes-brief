04 · What These Wireframes Are (and Aren't)

Frame the conversation, not the design.

What this is

A shared mental model so leadership can react to shape and scope before we invest in build.

What this isn't

Not final visuals. Not a tool selection (Power BI vs. Fabric vs. custom is downstream of agreeing on the views).

What we need from you

Reactions to: which views matter most, what's missing, and where leadership would actually start.
