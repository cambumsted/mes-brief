Phase 1 · Crawl — Define & Align

WorkstreamStatusDurationKey activities
Compile reports inventory Complete May Wk1–2 Catalog existing reports across channels — owner, cadence, audience, gaps.
Compile list of curated reports by channel In progress May Wk2 – Jul Wk1 From the broader inventory, identify the short list of reports each channel actually relies on for decisions — the curated set that the unified view should surface or replace.
Stakeholder discovery In progress May – Jun Interview DDS Marketing, channel leads, GTM, Finance — synthesize requirements list.
Marketing IQ capabilities discovery In progress May – end of Jun Map Marketing IQ's current and roadmap capabilities against requirements; go/no-go on integration.
Partnership grid template (v1) In progress May – Jun Wk2 Standardize cross-region partnership KPI grid; AMEX live by Jun Wk2.
Campaign-to-moment attribution In progress May – Aug Currently testing the addition of campaign classifications to clickthrough links on social & display. Establishes the data foundation to roll moments up in the unified view.
Paid media incrementality results & readout Milestone · Jul Wk1 Receive results, translate to leadership-ready readout — released Jul Wk1.
Share V1 AMEX partnership reporting template with RDS stakeholders Jun Wk3 – Jul Wk2 Walk RDS stakeholders through the V1 AMEX partnership reporting template — collect feedback and lock the format before expanding. Milestone Jul Wk2.
Assemble data sources for all other partnership types All of Jul Catalog the data sources for partnership types beyond AMEX so the V1 template can be applied to them in Walk.
Compile & share data sources for Marketing IQ In progress Jun Pull together the canonical data sources (channel, campaign, attribution, partnership) and hand them to the Marketing IQ team so they can wire them into the Outbound build.
Phase 1 exit:
  • Reports inventory complete and shared; curated-reports list assembled by channel.
  • Problem, goal, and direction shared with DDS LT (Jun Wk2) and agreed upon with leadership / end users.
  • Partnership grid MVP (AMEX) created and shared with regional partnerships leads.
  • V1 AMEX partnership reporting template shared with RDS stakeholders (Jul Wk2).
  • Data sources for all other partnership types assembled, ready for expansion in Walk.
  • Marketing IQ fit assessed — go/no-go on integration with the unified solution.
  • Marketing IQ data sources compiled and handed off (Jun) so Outbound development can start in Jul.
  • Paid media incrementality results released and digested (Jul Wk1).
  • Campaign-to-moment attribution foundation in place (clickthrough classifications live on social & display).

Phase 2 · Walk — Centralize & Standardize

WorkstreamStatusDurationKey activities
Development of Outbound Marketing IQ Jul – end of Aug Marketing IQ team builds the Outbound integration using the data sources handed over in Jun. First test of Marketing IQ with Outbound Marketing data targeted for end of Aug.
Expand manual partnerships reporting to additional partnership types Aug — ongoing Use the V1 AMEX template as a pattern and apply it to additional partnership types (beyond AMEX), keeping the format consistent so the rollups are comparable.
Phase 2 exit: Marketing IQ Outbound development complete with first test landed (end of Aug), manual partnerships reporting has expanded beyond AMEX to additional partnership types, and we are ready to move into integrated build and a steady operating rhythm in Run.

Phase 3 · Run — Integrate & Automate

WorkstreamStatusDurationKey activities
Adoption of Marketing IQ for Outbound Marketing Sep – Oct Stand up Marketing IQ as the working surface for Outbound Marketing post first-test — train users, lock the cadence, and incorporate feedback before broader rollout.
Operationalize partnership grid Quarterly cadence Regional contributors update the grid each quarter with a QA loop — now the steady-state operating rhythm.
Continuous trend view 4–6 weeks Always-on headline trends replace MBR-only cadence; alerting on directional shifts.
Embed in leadership rituals Ongoing Solution named as source of truth in MBR, planning, and investment conversations.
Phase 3 exit: the solution is leadership's first stop for marketing performance questions and is named as the source of truth in decision conversations.
