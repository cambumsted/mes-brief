Phase 1 · Crawl — Define & Align

Get to a shared baseline, organized by question

~8–10 weeks (May – mid-Jul) Goal: leadership can see one curated view of marketing performance, even if it's manually assembled — and every brief question has at least one workstream behind it.

Crawl coverage check vs. the brief's 5 questions

Every brief question now has at least one Crawl workstream feeding it:

  • Q1 · Where to invest? — Stakeholder discovery, Marketing IQ, Tracking education, Manual Unified View MVP.
  • Q2 · What moved the trend? — Tracking education, Marketing IQ, Manual Unified View MVP.
  • Q3 · Partnership ROI? — Partnership grid template (AMEX live by Jun Wk2).
  • Q4 · Campaign moment rollup? — Campaign moment inventory (new) scopes the moments so Walk can attribute against them.
  • Q5 · Paid media incrementality? — Incrementality test scoping (new) sets up the end-of-July paid media incrementality readout in Walk.

Pre-rewrite gap: the original Crawl was strong on foundations (discovery, tracking, partnerships) but had no workstream pointing at Q4 (campaign moments) or Q5 (paid media incrementality). The two new workstreams above close those gaps and create the runway for Walk-phase deliverables to land on time.

Phase 1 exit:
  • Problem, goal, and direction vocalized and agreed upon with leadership / end users.
  • Partnership grid MVP (AMEX) created and shared with regional partnerships leads.
  • Tracking capabilities outlined and path forward defined for answering wish-list questions.
  • Marketing IQ fit assessed — go/no-go on integration with the unified solution.
  • Incrementality test scoped and ready to land results end of July.
  • Priority campaign moments inventoried, ready for moment-attribution in Walk.
  • Stakeholders / RACI established.

Phase 2 · Walk — Centralize & Standardize

Move from curated artifact to operational rhythm

~3–4 months (Aug – Oct, with key results landing end of Jul) Goal: a real landing surface, regional contributors filling the grid on cadence, first moment-level attribution, and the first incrementality readout.

Tracking & Taxonomy Q1Q2Q4

WorkstreamDurationKey activitiesAnswers
Update OCID classifications 8–10 weeks Apply what we learned in the Crawl-phase deep dive: reclassify channels and sub-channels in the CMM hierarchy so reporting reflects how we actually market. Examples: split PMAX out from SEM as its own channel, formalize partnerships as a top-level classification, and align email/paid media taxonomies. Coordinate with the OCID request owners so all new requests follow the updated structure. Milestone: reclassification live in CMM by Sep Wk4. Q1Q2Q4

Partnerships Q3

WorkstreamDurationKey activitiesAnswers

WorkstreamDurationKey activitiesAnswers

Translate into a leadership-ready readout: lift by region/channel, holdout vs. exposed, confidence intervals, and recommendations for the next planning cycle. Milestone: paid media incrementality results released end of July. Q5

Phase 2 exit: updated OCID classifications live in CMM, the partnership grid is on a quarterly cadence with regional contributors, the paid media incrementality readout is in leadership's hands, and a first pass at moment-level attribution is rolled up in the Manual Unified View.

Phase 3 · Run — Integrate & Automate

Make it the source of truth

~4–6 months Goal: integrated solution with drill-downs, selective automation, and a permanent place in leadership decision rituals.
WorkstreamDurationKey activities
Integrated solution build 3–5 months Move from links-and-curation to integrated drill-downs across the four pillars. Form follows requirements — dashboard, hub, or hybrid.
Automate moment attribution 6–8 weeks Replace manual tagging with system-side rules where the data supports it. Keep human review for ambiguous attribution.
Continuous trend view 4–6 weeks Replace MBR-only cadence with always-on headline trends. Alerting on directional shifts before they become surprises.
Embed in leadership rituals Ongoing Solution is referenced by name in MBR, planning, and investment decisions. Adoption — not output — becomes the success metric.
Selective automation Continuous Automate where the ROI of automation is justified. Leave manual processes manual where they're cheaper than the integration cost.
Phase 3 exit: the solution is leadership's first stop for marketing performance questions and is named as the source of truth in decision conversations.
