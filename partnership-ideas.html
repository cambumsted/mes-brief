AMEX V1 MVP

A focused build for AMEX across Americas, Asia, and EMEA — the path we'll walk and the grid we'll fill.

The path

1 Define

Set the AMEX KPI list per region

  • Lock the KPIs we'll report on for AMEX in Americas, Asia, and EMEA.
  • Mark each as primary or secondary based on the region's goal.
2 Align

Confirm definitions are comparable

  • Pressure-test that ROI / Conversions / Traffic mean the same thing in every region.
  • Align on currency, attribution window, and what counts as a conversion.
  • Catch this before locating cells — saves rework downstream.
3 Locate

Find each KPI in regional reports

  • For every KPI × region cell, pinpoint the exact source.
  • Example: "EMEA AMEX ROI → EMEA Partnership Monthly, tab 3, cell D14."
  • Cross-reference against the Reports inventory.
4 Socialize

Share with RDS Partnerships teams

  • Walk RDS through the AMEX MVP.
  • Frame it as the model for every partnership going forward.
  • Gather feedback on definitions, feasibility, and ownership.
5 Standardize

Template for all partnerships

  • Bake learnings into one template every RDS Partnerships team fills out.
  • Include KPI definitions, source-of-truth pointers, and refresh cadence.
