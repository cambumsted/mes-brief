Path to AMEX V1 MVP

How we get from "AMEX is reported differently in every region" to one comparable view that becomes the template for every other partnership. Yellow steps are ones I think are worth adding to the four you sketched out.

1 Define

Set the AMEX KPI list per region

Lock the KPIs we'll report on for AMEX in Americas, Asia, and EMEA — including which one is the primary read for each region's goal (e.g., ROI for conversion regions, Traffic for traffic regions).

2 Suggested add Align

Confirm definitions are comparable

Before we go hunting in reports — pressure-test that "ROI", "Conversions", "Traffic" mean the same thing in EMEA as they do in Americas (currency, attribution window, what counts as a conversion).

3 Locate

Find each KPI in regional reports

For every KPI × region cell in the AMEX grid, pinpoint the exact source — e.g., "EMEA AMEX ROI lives in the EMEA Partnership Monthly, tab 3, cell D14." Cross-reference against the Reports inventory.

4 Suggested add Pilot

Manually fill the AMEX V1 MVP

Populate the grid with one full cycle of real numbers ourselves — proves the workflow end to end and gives us a working artifact (not just a template) to show RDS before asking them to adopt it.

5 Socialize

Share with RDS Partnerships teams

Walk RDS through the AMEX MVP. Frame it as the model for every partnership going forward, gather feedback on KPI definitions and feasibility, and set expectations on cadence + ownership.

6 Standardize

Standardized template for all partnerships

Bake the learnings into a single template that every RDS Partnerships team fills out for their partnerships, with clear KPI definitions, source-of-truth pointers, and a fixed refresh cadence.

Owner: Cam + DDS Goal: AMEX MVP first cycle Open: who from RDS leads adoption?

Yellow-bordered steps (2 & 4) are additions worth considering. Step 2 saves a lot of pain later — without it we risk cataloging metrics that aren't actually comparable. Step 4 turns the template into a real artifact before we ask anyone to adopt it.
